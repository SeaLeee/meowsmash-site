常见问题 FAQ

购买后没到账？ 设置 → 恢复购买。
Purchases missing? Settings → Restore Purchases.

如何去掉广告？ 商店购买“去广告”或新手礼包。
Remove ads? Buy "No Ads" or the Starter Pack in the Shop.

进度会丢失吗？ 进度保存在本机，请勿在换机前卸载。
Progress? Saved on device; don't delete the app before switching phones.

操作不顺手？ 设置里可调节拖动灵敏度，关闭震屏和闪光。
Controls? Adjust drag sensitivity and turn off shake/flash in Settings.
