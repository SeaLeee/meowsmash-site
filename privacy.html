概述

《喵击球》（“本应用”）尊重并保护你的隐私。本应用无需注册账号，游戏进度仅保存在你的设备本地，我们不运营任何收集个人信息的服务器。

我们处理的数据

  • 游戏进度与设置：仅保存在本机，卸载即删除。
  • 内购：通过 Apple App Store（StoreKit）完成，支付信息由 Apple 处理，我们无法获取。
  • Game Center：如你登录 Game Center，排行榜分数与成就由 Apple 处理。
  • 广告（Google AdMob）：为展示激励视频与插屏广告，AdMob 可能收集设备标识符（IDFA，仅在你通过“App 跟踪透明度”授权后）、粗略位置、设备与使用信息，用于广告投放、频次控制与反作弊。详见 Google 隐私政策。你可随时在 iOS 设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪 中关闭授权；购买“去广告”可移除插屏广告。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童定向收集个人信息。

Overview

Meow Smash ("the App") requires no account. Game progress is stored only on your device; we run no servers that collect personal data.

Data

  • Progress & settings: on-device only, removed when you delete the app.
  • Purchases: handled by Apple via StoreKit; we never see payment details.
  • Game Center: leaderboards and achievements are processed by Apple.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): to serve rewarded and interstitial ads, AdMob may collect device identifiers (IDFA only with your App Tracking Transparency permission), approximate location, device and usage data for ad delivery, frequency capping and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy. You can revoke tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; "Remove Ads" removes interstitials.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
